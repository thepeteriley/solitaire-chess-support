PRIVACY

Your game stays
on your device.

Solitaire Chess does not collect or transmit your games, positions, engine analysis, or personal information to the developer.

Developer: Pete Riley · Effective November 2, 2026

The app

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or remote backend. Play and engine analysis work offline using Stockfish on your device. Apple versions bundle the engine; the Linux edition uses the distribution’s Stockfish package.

Local progress and preferences

Your game, moves, playing mode, board orientation, chosen colour, and computer strength are saved locally to restore your session. This information is not sent to the developer or shared by the app with third parties.

Starting a new game replaces the saved game. Changing a preference replaces the saved value for that preference.

Apple’s services

App Store transactions and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple’s policies and your system settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

This support website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded trackers, contact forms, or cookies added by the developer. GitHub may process technical information about visits as part of operating its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Support requests

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. Posting requires a GitHub account. Requests and any attachments are public, so do not include passwords, Apple Account details, private keys, personal documents, or other private information. Information you choose to post is used to respond to your request and improve the app.

Questions about privacy

Contact Pete Riley through the support tracker. If a question requires private information, request a private contact route first without posting the information publicly.

Policy changes

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. This policy describes the app’s current behavior; a future release that changes its data practices will require an updated policy.
